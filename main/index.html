About IRISTEM

We make serious science feel possible.

IRISTEM is a student-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit connecting advanced research with younger learners.

Our student perspective helps researchers turn complex ideas into clear curriculum, hands-on activities, visual stories, and outreach that meets young people where they are.
